# Parmar Packaging — Business Website

> A modern, responsive business website developed for **Parmar Packaging**, a packaging-material manufacturer and wholesale supplier based in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

**[Visit Parmar Packaging](https://parmarpackaging.com/)**

---

## 📌 Overview

**Parmar Packaging** provides a wide range of plastic and industrial packaging materials for manufacturers, traders, exporters, retailers, e-commerce businesses, and other commercial clients.

This website was designed to provide:

- 🏢 A professional online presence for the business
- 📦 Clear presentation of available packaging products
- 🔎 Easy product discovery through categories
- 💬 Direct customer communication through WhatsApp
- 📞 Quick quote and enquiry options
- 🛍️ A dedicated product catalogue

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Business Landing Page

The homepage includes:

- Hero section
- Company introduction
- Product highlights
- Business statistics
- Key advantages
- Contact / quote section
- Footer with business information

### 📦 Product Showcase

The homepage highlights selected products including:

- **LD Bags**
- **HM Bags**
- **PP Woven Fabric Bags**
- **Stretch Film**
- **Courier Bags**
- **BOPP Adhesive Tape**

Users can access the complete catalogue through the **View All Products** section.

### 🗂️ Complete Product Catalogue

The dedicated products page provides a larger catalogue with category-based filtering.

Available categories include:

- **All Products**
- **Bags**
- **Films & Wraps**
- **Tapes**
- **Strapping**
- **E-commerce**
- **Custom Printing**
- **Textile Packaging**
- **Other Products**

This allows visitors to quickly find the type of packaging product they need.

### 💬 Direct Quote Requests

Customers can contact the business directly through:

- 📱 **WhatsApp**
- ☎️ **Phone Call**
- 💰 **Request a Quote**

The website provides direct WhatsApp and phone links for quick enquiries.

### 📱 Responsive Navigation

The website includes navigation for:

- Home
- About Us
- Products
- Why Choose Us
- Contact Us

A mobile navigation menu is also implemented for smaller screens.

### 🖼️ Product Images

Product and business imagery is stored locally inside the `images` directory and is used throughout the website to showcase the company's products and branding.
